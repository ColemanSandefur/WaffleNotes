# Who Is God?

*"The eternal God is your refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms."* — Deuteronomy 33:27

One of the most important questions anyone can ask is, "Who is God?" Our answer shapes how we live, worship, and prepare for eternity. Rather than relying on human opinions or traditions, we must turn to the Bible, where God reveals Himself.

## God Is the Eternal Creator

The Bible begins with these words:

> "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth." (Genesis 1:1)

Before there was time, space, or matter, God already existed. He is not a created being. He is eternal, self-existent, and the source of all life.

Psalm 90:2 declares:

> "Before the mountains were brought forth... from everlasting to everlasting, You are God."

Everything we see points to His creative power. The universe did not come into existence by accident. It reflects the wisdom and glory of its Creator (Psalm 19:1; Romans 1:20).

## God Is One

The Scriptures consistently teach that there is only one true God.

"Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one!" (Deuteronomy 6:4)

The apostle Paul affirmed the same truth:

> "There is one God and Father of all, who is above all, and through all, and in you all." (Ephesians 4:6)

Christians reject the worship of idols because only the living God deserves honor, praise, and obedience.

## God Reveals Himself as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit

While there is only one God, the Bible reveals the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

At Jesus' baptism, all three are present. Jesus is baptized, the Holy Spirit descends like a dove, and the Father speaks from heaven (Matthew 3:16–17).

Jesus commanded His disciples:

> "Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit." (Matthew 28:19)

The Father is God. Jesus Christ is God in the flesh (John 1:1, 14). The Holy Spirit is also divine (Acts 5:3–4). Though the Bible does not explain every aspect of God's nature, it clearly reveals these truths, and we accept what Scripture teaches.

## God's Character

God is not only powerful; He is perfectly holy, righteous, loving, and faithful.

- **God is holy** (Isaiah 6:3).
- **God is love** (1 John 4:8).
- **God is just** (Deuteronomy 32:4).
- **God is merciful** (Psalm 103:8).
- **God is faithful** (Lamentations 3:22–23).

His holiness means He cannot tolerate sin. His justice requires that sin be punished. Yet His love moved Him to provide salvation through Jesus Christ.

Romans 5:8 reminds us:

> "But God demonstrates His own love toward us, in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us."

## God Desires a Relationship with Us

God created humanity in His image (Genesis 1:26–27). Although sin separated us from Him (Isaiah 59:2), He desires our reconciliation.

Through the gospel, God invites every person to receive forgiveness. Jesus said:

> "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me." (John 14:6)

Those who hear the gospel, believe in Christ, repent of their sins, confess Jesus as Lord, and are baptized into Christ receive the forgiveness of sins and become children of God (Mark 16:16; Acts 2:38; Romans 10:9–10; Galatians 3:26–27).

## How Should We Respond?

Knowing who God is should change how we live.

- Worship Him in spirit and truth (John 4:24).
- Trust Him completely (Proverbs 3:5–6).
- Obey His Word (John 14:15).
- Love Him with all your heart, soul, mind, and strength (Mark 12:30).
- Share His gospel with others (Matthew 28:18–20).

God is not distant or unknowable. He has revealed Himself through creation, through His inspired Word, and most fully through His Son, Jesus Christ.

## Conclusion

The God of the Bible is eternal, all-powerful, all-knowing, holy, loving, and faithful. He alone is worthy of our worship. He created us, sustains us, and offers salvation through Jesus Christ.

The greatest decision anyone can make is to seek God according to His Word and submit to His will. As Jeremiah wrote:

> "And you will seek Me and find Me, when you search for Me with all your heart." (Jeremiah 29:13)

May we continue to know God more deeply through faithful study of His Word and faithful obedience to His commands, giving Him the glory He alone deserves.
